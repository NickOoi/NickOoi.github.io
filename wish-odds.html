《最后一盏灯》华丽祈愿 · 概率公示

适用版本：6.7.0 起（Google Play 版和本站 APK 相同）· 现在在卖的祈愿池：凤羽极光 · 更新日期：2026 年 11 月 16 日

每次祈愿用 1 张灯愿券；券不够时可以勾选用钻石代付，每张 30 颗。下面的机会和游戏里祈愿页「概率」页签（抽卡按钮旁的「概率公示」）显示的一致，抽之前就能看到。

保底

这一池连续 59 次没出典藏，第 60 次必出（最多 60 次一定拿到）；次数跟着这一池一直记着（每个祈愿池各自计数，换池不清），出了典藏（含重复）才从头算；十连按 10 次逐次计。

含保底的综合概率：典藏约 2.52%（平均约 39.7 次出一次）。

重复怎么算

这一池的典藏已经有了再抽到，换 150 星屑（可在「星屑兑换」换别的），照样算出了典藏、保底从头算；专属拖尾已经有了再抽到，换 40 星屑。

专属环绕和称号怎么拿

这一池的专属环绕和称号不在上面「每次祈愿的机会」里，用下面两种方式拿到（每池各一次）：

  • 「星屑兑换」：专属环绕 160 星屑、称号 80 星屑
  • 「累计奖励」：这一池累计祈愿满 120 次送专属环绕，满 240 次送称号（另加灯芯 ×10）

幸运盒的概率（飞机皮肤 / 怪物时装）

幸运盒也是随机获得，每盒 150 钻石：一盒开 3 件互不相同的，第 1 件一定是你还没有的；重复的换碎片（普通款 50、隐藏款 150）。

每件开到的机会按你现在的收藏算：份数越多越容易抽到，本周新款的份数是普通款的 2 倍，所以每个人、每一周都不一样。开之前在游戏商城幸运盒的「概率」页签能看到每一件开到的机会，卡片上也写着这一盒开到隐藏款的机会。

此為機會中獎商品，消費者購買或參與活動不代表即可獲得特定商品。

The Last Lamp · Spectacular Wishes · Odds

Applies to version 6.7.0 and later (the Google Play version and this site's APK are the same) · Current wish pool: Phoenix Aurora · Updated: 16 November 2026

Each wish uses 1 Wish Ticket; if you are short, you can tick a box to pay with Diamonds instead, 30 per ticket. The odds below match the "Odds" tab on the wish page in the game (also opened by the "Odds" button next to the wish buttons), shown before you draw.

The items in a tier share that tier's chance equally; figures are rounded to two decimals (for example 4 × 16.88% = 67.52% in the Common tier; the actual chance is 67.5%).

Guarantee

If 59 wishes in a row in this pool miss the Treasure, wish number 60 gives it (you get it within 60 wishes at most). The count stays with the pool (each wish pool counts separately; switching pools keeps it) and resets only when you get the Treasure (duplicates count). A 10-wish counts as 10 single wishes.

Overall Treasure rate including the guarantee: about 2.52% (about once every 39.7 wishes on average).

Duplicates

If you already own this pool's Treasure and get it again, it becomes 150 Stardust (spend it in the Stardust shop); it still counts as a Treasure, so the guarantee count starts over. An exclusive trail you already own becomes 40 Stardust.

Exclusive orbit and title

This pool's exclusive orbit and title are not part of the "Odds per wish" above. You get them in these two ways (once per pool):

  • Stardust shop: exclusive orbit 160 Stardust, title 80 Stardust
  • Milestones: 120 wishes in this pool give the exclusive orbit; 240 wishes give the title (plus 10 Wicks)

Lucky Box odds (plane skins / monster costumes)

Lucky Boxes also give random items, 150 Diamonds per box: each box holds 3 different pieces, and the first one is always new to you. Duplicates turn into shards (50 each, 150 for hidden pieces).

The chance of each piece is based on what you own now: pieces with more shares come up more often, and pieces new this week have twice the shares of regular ones, so the odds differ by player and by week. Before you open one, the "Odds" tab of the Lucky Box in the game shop shows the chance of every piece, and the card shows this box's chance of a hidden piece.

Wishes are a chance-based item: making a purchase or taking part does not guarantee any specific item.
