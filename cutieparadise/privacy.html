《萌宝乐园》隐私政策

开发者：森澜岛游戏 · 适用应用：萌宝乐园（Android，包名 com.cutieparadise.game，以及网页版）· 生效日期：2026 年 10 月 12 日

1. 我们收集什么、为什么

《萌宝乐园》是联网游戏：你的萌宝和进度保存在我们的服务器上，这样换手机、重装也能接着玩，也能防止作弊。我们不需要、也不收集你的姓名、电话、邮箱、通讯录、照片或位置。

数据用途保留多久
游客账号：第一次打开游戏时，手机随机生成的“安装编号”，和我们发给手机的登录凭证（服务器只存凭证的摘要）识别你的账号、让你保持登录直到你删除账号；登录凭证 180 天不用自动失效
游戏数据：萌宝、队伍、关卡进度、金币和萌晶、你自己起的训练师名字、领奖和任务记录提供游戏功能直到你删除账号
战斗记录：每场战斗的随机种子和你的操作（移珠路线、放技能）在服务器上核对胜负，防止作弊结算后 30 天自动删除
购买记录：通过 Google Play 购买萌晶时，Google 给我们的购买凭证（只存摘要）、订单号、商品；萌晶增减流水核对付款、发放萌晶、处理退款和对账长期保存（财务对账需要）；删除账号后不再和任何游戏账号身份关联使用
服务器日志：访问时间、IP 地址、请求的功能和结果安全（防刷、限制频率）和排查故障定期轮转覆盖，不用于其他目的

付款由 Google Play 处理，我们看不到你的银行卡或其他付款信息。

2. 没有广告、没有第三方追踪

游戏里没有广告，也没有接入任何第三方统计、广告或追踪工具。我们不出售、不出租你的数据，也不和第三方共享，法律要求的情况除外。

3. 分享

如果你自己点了游戏里的“分享”，游戏会在手机上生成一张萌宝的图片和一段带链接的文字，交给你选择的应用（例如 Facebook、WhatsApp）；发不发、发给谁由你决定。分享内容只有游戏画面和萌宝信息，之后由那个应用按它自己的隐私政策处理。我们的分享页面（nickooi.github.io/cutieparadise/share/）只用来显示链接卡片并跳转到 Google Play，不统计访问、不放广告。

4. 数据保存与安全

数据保存在我们租用的服务器上，手机和服务器之间全程 HTTPS 加密传输；服务器每天备份（备份保留 7 天）。只有开发者能管理服务器，运营后台只用来调整游戏内容（怪物、关卡、活动），不能查看或修改玩家个人数据。

5. 删除账号

你可以随时删除账号：游戏里 设置 → 删除账号（点两次确认），账号、游戏数据和登录凭证会立刻删除，不能恢复。也可以在网页上申请：删除账号说明。删除后只保留第 1 条里的购买记录用于对账。

卸载游戏不会删除服务器上的账号；如果手机开启了 Android 系统备份，登录凭证可能被备份到你自己的 Google 账号，重装后会回到原来的账号。

6. 儿童

本游戏不以 13 岁以下儿童为目标用户。我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息；如果你认为我们误收了儿童的信息，请联系我们删除。

7. 政策更新和联系我们

政策有变化时，我们会更新本页面并修改上方的生效日期。有任何问题，请通过 Google Play 商店本应用页面“开发者联系方式”里的邮箱联系我们。

Cutie Paradise — Privacy Policy

Developer: 森澜岛游戏 · App: Cutie Paradise (Android, package com.cutieparadise.game, and the web version) · Effective date: October 12, 2026

1. What we collect and why

Cutie Paradise is an online game: your cuties and progress are stored on our server so you can keep playing after changing or reinstalling your phone, and to prevent cheating. We do not need or collect your name, phone number, email, contacts, photos or location.

DataPurposeRetention
Guest account: a random "install ID" your phone creates the first time you open the game, and the sign-in token we give it (the server stores only a hash of the token)Identify your account and keep you signed inUntil you delete your account; tokens unused for 180 days expire
Game data: cuties, teams, stage progress, coins and gems, the trainer name you choose, reward and task recordsProvide the gameUntil you delete your account
Battle records: each battle's random seed and your moves (rune paths, skills)Verify results on the server to prevent cheatingDeleted automatically 30 days after the battle ends
Purchase records: when you buy gems through Google Play, the purchase token Google gives us (stored as a hash), order ID and product; gem balance historyVerify payment, deliver gems, handle refunds and accountingKept long-term for accounting; after you delete your account they are no longer used with any game identity
Server logs: time, IP address, the feature requested and the resultSecurity (abuse prevention, rate limiting) and troubleshootingRotated regularly; not used for anything else

Payments are processed by Google Play. We never see your card or other payment details.

2. No ads, no third-party tracking

The game shows no ads and contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools. We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, except where required by law.

3. Sharing

If you tap "Share" in the game, it creates a picture of a cutie and a short text with a link on your phone and hands them to the app you choose (for example Facebook or WhatsApp); you decide whether and to whom to send them. The content contains only game visuals and cutie information, and is then handled by that app under its own privacy policy. Our share pages (nickooi.github.io/cutieparadise/share/) only show the link preview and redirect to Google Play; they do not track visits or show ads.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored on servers we rent. Traffic between your phone and the server is encrypted with HTTPS, and the server is backed up daily (backups are kept for 7 days). Only the developer administers the server; the operations console is used only to adjust game content (monsters, stages, events) and cannot view or change players' personal data.

5. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the game: Settings → Delete account (tap twice to confirm). Your account, game data and sign-in tokens are deleted immediately and cannot be restored. You can also request deletion on the web: how to delete your account. After deletion only the purchase records in section 1 are kept for accounting.

Uninstalling the game does not delete your account on the server. If Android system backup is on, your sign-in token may be backed up to your own Google account and you will return to the same account after reinstalling.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; if you believe we have, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update this page and the effective date above. For any question, please email us at the address listed under "Developer contact" on this app's Google Play store page.
