《萌宝乐园》删除账号

开发者：森澜岛游戏 · 应用：萌宝乐园（Android，com.cutieparadise.game）

方法一：在游戏里删除（马上生效）

  1. 打开游戏，在大厅点右上方工具条的 设置。
  2. 点 删除账号，看清提示后再点一次 删除 确认。

方法二：发邮件申请

不能打开游戏的话，请发邮件到 Google Play 商店本应用页面“开发者联系方式”里的邮箱，标题写“删除账号”，并附上你的账号编号（游戏里 设置 页最下面能看到）。我们核对后会在 7 天内删除，并回邮件告诉你。

会删除什么、保留什么

  • 立刻删除：游客账号（安装编号）、登录凭证、全部游戏数据（萌宝、队伍、进度、金币和萌晶、训练师名字、任务和领奖记录）。删除后不能恢复，买过的萌晶也会一起消失。
  • 按时自动删除：操作记录（防重复用）7 天后删除，战斗记录在战斗结束 30 天后删除，服务器日志定期轮转覆盖；服务器的每日备份里还留有的数据，最多 7 天后随备份一起删除。
  • 保留：Google Play 购买记录（订单号、商品、购买凭证摘要）和萌晶增减流水，用于财务对账和处理退款，不再和任何游戏账号身份关联使用。

详见 隐私政策。

Cutie Paradise — Delete your account

Developer: 森澜岛游戏 · App: Cutie Paradise (Android, com.cutieparadise.game)

Option 1: in the game (immediate)

  1. Open the game and tap Settings in the toolbar at the top of the lobby.
  2. Tap Delete account, read the warning, then tap Delete again to confirm.

Option 2: by email

If you cannot open the game, email the address listed under "Developer contact" on this app's Google Play page with the subject "Delete account", and include your account number (shown at the bottom of the in-game Settings). We will delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted immediately: your guest account (install ID), sign-in tokens, and all game data (cuties, teams, progress, coins and gems, trainer name, task and reward records). This cannot be undone, and purchased gems are lost too.
  • Deleted on schedule: operation records (used to prevent duplicates) after 7 days, battle records 30 days after each battle, and server logs are rotated regularly; copies in the server's daily backups are removed with those backups within 7 days.
  • Kept: Google Play purchase records (order ID, product, hashed purchase token) and gem balance history, for accounting and refunds; they are no longer used with any game identity.

See the privacy policy.
