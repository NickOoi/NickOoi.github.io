《萌兽小岛》账号与数据删除说明

开发者：森澜岛游戏 · 适用应用：萌兽小岛（Android，包名 com.petisland.game）

方式一：在游戏里自己删（立即生效）

  1. 打开游戏，点底部的「好友」。
  2. 点面板最下方的「删除账号」，确认。
  3. 服务器会立即永久删除：宠物存档、金币和物品、好友关系、登录凭证，以及（如果绑定过）Facebook 用户编号和名字。手机上的本地存档也会重置。操作不可恢复。

方式二：联系我们代为删除

如果游戏已经卸载或无法登录，请通过 Google Play 商店页面上的开发者邮箱联系我们，邮件标题写「萌兽小岛 删除账号」，并提供游戏里的好友码（6 位）或你用来登录的 Facebook 名字。我们会在 7 天内删除并回复确认。

Facebook 用户

你也可以在 Facebook 的「设置 → 应用和网站」里移除「萌兽小岛」；Meta 会通知我们，我们会删除与该 Facebook 账号关联的数据。

删除范围与保留

删除后我们不再保留任何可识别你的数据。服务器访问日志（含 IP 地址）按安全需要最多保留 30 天后自动覆盖。

Pet Island — Account & Data Deletion

Developer: Senlan Island Games · App: Pet Island (Android, package com.petisland.game)

Option 1: Delete in the game (immediate)

  1. Open the game and tap Friends at the bottom.
  2. Tap Delete account at the bottom of the panel and confirm.
  3. The server immediately and permanently deletes your pet save, coins and items, friendships, login credentials and (if linked) your Facebook user ID and name. The local save on the phone is reset too. This cannot be undone.

Option 2: Ask us to delete

If the game is uninstalled or you cannot sign in, email the developer address shown on our Google Play store page with the subject "Pet Island delete account" and include your 6-character friend code or the Facebook name you used. We will delete the data and confirm within 7 days.

Facebook users

You can also remove "Pet Island" under Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites; Meta notifies us and we delete the data associated with that Facebook account.

Scope and retention

After deletion we keep no data that identifies you. Server access logs (including IP addresses) are kept for security for at most 30 days and then overwritten.
