《萌兽小岛》隐私政策

开发者：森澜岛游戏 · 适用应用：萌兽小岛（Android，包名 com.petisland.game）· 生效日期：2026-11-02

1. 我们收集什么、为什么

《萌兽小岛》是一款养宠物的联网游戏：你的宠物和进度保存在我们的服务器上，这样换手机、重装也能接着玩，也能防止修改手机时间作弊。我们不收集你的姓名、电话、邮箱、通讯录或位置，除非你自己选择用 Facebook 登录（见第 2 条）。

数据用途保留多久
游客账号：第一次打开游戏时，手机随机生成的账号编号，和我们发给手机的登录凭证（服务器只存凭证的摘要）认出是同一个玩家，保存进度到你删除账号
游戏数据：宠物名字、数值、形态、金币、买过的物品、小岛装饰、好友码、好友列表提供游戏功能，换手机接回进度到你删除账号
服务器日志：访问时间、IP 地址、请求的功能和结果安全（防刷、限制频率）和排查故障定期轮转覆盖，最长 30 天

2. Facebook 登录（可选）

你可以选择用 Facebook 登录，用来换手机时接回存档，以及找到也在玩这个游戏的 Facebook 好友。这时我们会从 Meta 收到并保存：你的 Facebook 用户编号（仅限本应用）和公开名字；同步好友时，我们只保存那些也在玩本游戏的好友的 Facebook 用户编号，用来把你们加为游戏好友。我们不会获取你的 Facebook 密码、帖子、照片或完整好友名单，也不会代你发布任何内容。

Facebook 登录受 Meta 隐私政策约束。你可以在游戏「好友」面板或 Facebook 的「应用和网站」设置里随时断开。

3. 没有广告、没有第三方追踪

游戏里没有广告，也没有接入任何广告或分析 SDK。除了第 2 条的 Facebook 登录，我们不把你的数据提供给任何第三方。

4. 手机上的权限

通知：用来在宠物饿了、生病了的时候提醒你，由手机本地定时触发，不经过服务器。你可以在系统设置里关闭。网络：用来同步存档和好友。游戏不要求其他权限。

5. 儿童

游戏面向一般玩家。我们不会有意收集 13 岁以下儿童的个人信息；如果你认为我们误收了，请联系我们删除。

6. 你的权利：查看、删除

你可以随时在游戏里「好友 → 删除账号」删除账号：服务器上的宠物存档、好友关系、登录凭证和（如果绑定过）Facebook 关联会立即、永久删除，不可恢复。也可以按账号与数据删除说明联系我们代为删除。

7. 数据安全与存放

数据存放在我们租用的云服务器上。测试期间游戏与服务器之间的连接可能使用未加密的 HTTP；正式上架前会全部改为 HTTPS 加密传输。登录凭证在服务器上只保存摘要。

8. 联系我们

关于隐私的问题，请通过 Google Play 商店页面上的开发者邮箱联系我们。

9. 政策更新

本政策有变动时，我们会更新本页面并改动生效日期；重大变动会在游戏内提示。

Pet Island Privacy Policy

Developer: Senlan Island Games · App: Pet Island (Android, package com.petisland.game) · Effective: 2026-11-02

1. What we collect and why

Pet Island is an online pet-raising game. Your pet and progress are stored on our server so you can continue on a new phone and so the game clock cannot be cheated. We do not collect your name, phone number, email, contacts or location unless you choose Facebook Login (section 2).

DataPurposeRetention
Guest account: a random account ID generated on your phone on first launch, and the login credential we issue (server keeps only a hash)Recognise the same player, save progressUntil you delete your account
Game data: pet name, stats, form, coins, purchased items, island decorations, friend code, friend listProvide the game, restore progress on a new phoneUntil you delete your account
Server logs: time, IP address, requested feature and resultSecurity (abuse prevention, rate limiting) and troubleshootingRotated, at most 30 days

2. Facebook Login (optional)

You may sign in with Facebook to restore your save on a new phone and to find Facebook friends who also play this game. We then receive and store your app-scoped Facebook user ID and public name. When syncing friends we store only the app-scoped IDs of friends who also play this game, to connect you in the game. We never receive your Facebook password, posts, photos or full friend list, and we never post on your behalf. Facebook Login is governed by the Meta Privacy Policy; you can disconnect at any time from the game's Friends panel or Facebook's "Apps and Websites" settings.

3. No ads, no third-party tracking

The game contains no ads and no advertising or analytics SDKs. Apart from Facebook Login, we do not share your data with third parties.

4. Device permissions

Notifications: local reminders when your pet is hungry or sick, scheduled on the device, not via our server. Network: syncing saves and friends. No other permissions are requested.

5. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe we have and we will delete it.

6. Your rights: access and deletion

Delete your account anytime in the game via Friends → Delete account: your pet save, friendships, login credentials and (if linked) Facebook association are permanently deleted immediately. You may also follow the account and data deletion instructions.

7. Security and storage

Data is stored on cloud servers we rent. During testing the connection between the game and the server may use unencrypted HTTP; before public release all traffic will use HTTPS. Login credentials are stored only as hashes.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer email shown on our Google Play store page.

9. Changes

We will update this page and the effective date when this policy changes; material changes will be announced in the game.
